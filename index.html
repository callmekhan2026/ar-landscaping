Why ours stay flat

Most of a good driveway is the part you never see.

Massachusetts ground freezes and thaws over and over every winter. A driveway on a thin base rises and sinks with it, and that is where cracks, ruts and loose pavers come from.

We dig out, lay fabric, build up and compact a real gravel base, then set the surface on top. It takes more labor up front and saves you from paying for it twice.

Questions

What homeowners ask before they call.

How much does a new driveway or patio cost?

It depends on the size, the material you choose (pavers, natural stone or concrete) and how much base and drainage work your property needs. We walk the property for free and give you one written price before any work starts.

Why do driveways crack and sink in Massachusetts?

The ground here freezes and thaws over and over each winter. A driveway on a thin base moves with it. We dig out, lay fabric and compact a real gravel base so the surface stays flat.

How long does a driveway or patio take?

It depends on the size of the job and how much excavation and base work it needs. You get a timeline with your written estimate, and the same crew handles it start to finish.

Do I need a permit for a retaining wall?

Taller walls often need a building permit and sometimes an engineer's design, and the rules vary by town. We check what your town requires before we build.

Can you fix water pooling next to my foundation?

Yes. We regrade the yard so water runs away from the house and add drainage stone where it's needed, before it turns into a wet basement.

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. There's no fee for the visit and no pressure. You get one written price, and you decide.
